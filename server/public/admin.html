Material Demand Forecast

Every open (not-yet-complete) batch, grouped by Material — total Sheet Qty still needed, how many batches are contributing, and which Jobs they belong to. For purchasing decisions before running short on stock.

Stalled Batches

Open batches with no scan activity in the last several working days (weekends excluded) — a batch that's stopped moving, whether mid-progress or never started.

At Risk

Batches that are behind pace or overdue — under 20% complete with 70%+ of the time to Target Finish already gone, or past Target Finish and still incomplete. Same red rows as Production Schedule, surfaced here on their own.

Throughput & Yield Analytics

Completed-batch cycle time and completion rate by Material, Finish, and Job, plus Sheet Qty vs. parts-produced yield by Material. Cycle time and yield figures only include batches that have actually finished — there's nothing to measure on ones still in progress.
